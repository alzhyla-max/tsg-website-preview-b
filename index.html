How it works

From “where do I start?” to sorted

Four steps. Most of the work is ours.

You mostly just talk. We do the paperwork.

  1. 01

    We check your ATO history

    With your permission we look up what the ATO already has: income statements, past lodgements, any debts or letters. No digging through drawers.

  2. 02

    A relaxed phone interview

    About 30 minutes. We go through your year (or years) in plain English and ask the questions that find the things people miss.

  3. 03

    Advice for your situation

    You hear what we found and what it means, before anything is lodged. Questions are welcome. There are no silly ones.

  4. 04

    Lodged, and you can relax

    We lodge with the ATO and keep you posted. If a refund is due, our fee can come out of it, so there is nothing to pay upfront.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Can't see yours? Call us. Asking costs nothing.

No silly questions. Really.

Do I need to come into an office?

No. Almost everything is done over the phone or Zoom, anywhere in Australia. If you prefer to meet in person, we can come to you in some areas.

How does the fee come out of my refund?

When a refund is due, the ATO can pay it to our trust account. We take the agreed fee and send the rest straight to you. If there is no refund, or you would rather pay directly, you can pay by card or bank transfer.

I haven't lodged for years. Will I get in trouble?

Most people find it is much less serious than they feared. We check your ATO records first so we know exactly what is outstanding. Where penalties apply we can ask the ATO to reduce them. You will know where you stand after the first call.

What do I need to have ready?

Not much. Your name, date of birth and address so we can find you in the ATO system, plus anything you already have, such as receipts or a logbook. We will tell you if anything else is needed.

How quickly can you lodge?

A simple tax return can often be lodged the same day as your appointment. Catch-ups for several years usually take one to two weeks, depending on what the ATO has on file.
