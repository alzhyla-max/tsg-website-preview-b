FAQ

Questions, answered simply.

Can't see your question? Call us. Asking costs nothing, and there are no silly questions.

General

Do I need to come into an office?

No. Almost everything is done over the phone or Zoom, anywhere in Australia.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday, 9am to 7pm, by appointment. Evenings and Saturdays cost the same as weekdays.

How does fee from refund work?

When a refund is due, the ATO pays it into our trust account. We take the agreed fee and send the rest to you. You can also pay directly if you prefer.

How do I send you documents safely?

Never through the website form. After your first call we set you up with a secure way to share documents.

Late tax returns

How many years back can I lodge?

There's no cut-off for lodging a late tax return. We have caught up clients who had not lodged since 2002. The ATO may only ask for certain years, and we check that with them first so you don't do more than you need to.

I've lost my payment summaries and receipts. Can you still help?

Yes. Employers, banks and Centrelink report most income to the ATO, and we can retrieve those records for you. For deductions, we work with what you have and explain what can still be claimed.

Will I be fined for lodging late?

Not always. The ATO can apply a failure-to-lodge penalty, but where you are owed refunds, or have a good reason, we can ask the ATO to reduce or remove it. We'll tell you where you stand after we check your records.

What if I owe the ATO money?

We'll tell you the amount before anything is lodged, and explain the options. The ATO offers payment plans, and we can help you set one up.

How long does it take?

Most catch-ups are lodged within one to two weeks of your phone interview. It depends on how many years there are and what the ATO already has on file.

Individual tax returns

Why use a tax agent instead of myGov?

myGov is fine for simple situations. A registered tax agent asks about the things people often miss, checks what the ATO has pre-filled and makes sure the claims you make are ones you are entitled to.

What do I need for the call?

Your name, date of birth and address so we can find you in the ATO system, plus any receipts, a logbook or notes about work expenses. We'll guide you through the rest.

Can I do it after hours?

Yes. Appointments run Monday to Saturday, 9am to 7pm, at no extra cost.

Rental property

Do I need a depreciation schedule?

Often it's worth it, especially for newer properties. We'll look at your property and tell you whether a schedule from a quantity surveyor is likely to be useful.

Can you help before I sell?

Yes, and that's the best time to talk. Selling can have capital gains tax consequences, and the timing and records matter. A short call beforehand can save a lot of guesswork.

Sole traders

Do I need to register for GST?

It depends mainly on your turnover. We'll look at your numbers and explain whether GST registration is required or worth it for you.

What if I haven't kept good records?

That's common in the first year or two. We'll work with your bank statements and what you have, and show you a simple way to keep track from now on.

Companies and trusts

My company has stopped trading. Does it still need to lodge?

Usually yes, until it is deregistered. We can lodge for an inactive company and explain the options for winding it up.

BAS and GST

Can you catch up overdue BAS?

Yes. We work out which periods are outstanding, prepare them in order and talk to the ATO about any penalties or payment plans if needed.

ATO penalties

Can a penalty really be reduced?

Sometimes. The ATO considers your circumstances and history. We can prepare a remission request that explains your situation clearly. We can't promise an outcome, but we'll tell you honestly whether it's worth asking.

I've been selected for an audit. What happens now?

Send us the letter. We'll explain what the ATO is asking for, help you gather it and deal with them on your behalf.

A free first call, no obligation and nothing to sign. Tell us where you are at and we will tell you the next step.
