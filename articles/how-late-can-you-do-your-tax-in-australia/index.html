How Late Can You Do Your Tax in Australia?

21 December 2023 7 min read By the Tax Solutions Group team

Draft note: imported unchanged from taxsolutionsgroup.com.au (first published 21 December 2023). To be reviewed and updated before launch.

Introduction to tax deadlines in Australia

When it comes to taxes, it’s crucial to be aware of the deadlines and ensure that you meet them. In Australia, the tax year runs from 1st July to 30th June, and individuals must lodge their tax returns by a specific due date. So, how late can you do your tax in Australia? The deadline for most individuals is 31st October following the end of the tax year. It’s important to understand the consequences of missing this deadline and the steps you can take if you find yourself filing your tax return late.

What happens if you miss the tax deadline?

Missing the tax deadline in Australia can lead to a series of consequences. The Australian Taxation Office (ATO) imposes late lodgement penalties and charges interest on any outstanding tax liabilities. The penalties vary depending on the time elapsed since the due date and the amount of tax owed. Initially, if you do not lodge your tax return by the due date, the ATO may issue a failure to lodge (FTL) penalty, which can be as high as $222 for each 28-day period after the deadline. Additionally, interest charges add on any outstanding tax amounts.

Late lodgement penalties and consequences

If you miss the tax deadline in Australia and do not lodge your tax return, the ATO can take further action. They may issue a default assessment, where they estimate your income and expenses and calculate your tax liability accordingly. This estimate may not accurately reflect your actual financial situation, potentially resulting in an overpayment or underpayment of tax. In the case of an underpayment, the ATO will charge interest on the outstanding amount.

Moreover, late lodgement can affect your access to government benefits and refunds. If you are entitled to any refunds or government aid, these may be delayed until your tax return is lodged. It’s crucial to understand that the longer you delay lodging your tax return, the more severe the consequences may become. Therefore, it’s essential to take immediate action if you have missed the tax deadline.

How to request an extension for filing your tax return

In certain circumstances, you may be eligible for an extension to file your tax return after the deadline. If you need additional time due to a genuine reason, such as illness, natural disaster, or other exceptional circumstances, you can request an extension from the ATO. To do so, you need to contact the ATO and explain your situation. They will assess your request on a case-by-case basis and may grant you an extension. It’s important to note that extensions are not always guaranteed, so it’s best to avoid relying on them whenever possible.

What happens if you haven’t filed a tax return in years?

If you haven’t filed a tax return in years, it’s crucial to take immediate action to rectify the situation. How late can you do your tax in Australia? Not lodging tax returns for multiple years can result in severe consequences. The ATO may issue default assessments based on their estimates of your income and expenses, which can lead to incorrect tax liabilities. Additionally, the ATO may impose higher penalties and further interest charges for non-compliance.

To address this situation, it’s crucial to lodge your outstanding tax returns as soon as possible. The ATO encourages individuals to come forward and take the necessary steps to rectify their tax affairs. Seeking professional advice from a tax agent or accountant can be beneficial in navigating the process and ensuring that you follow all tax obligations.

Steps to file your tax return after the deadline.

If you find yourself filing your tax return after the deadline, here are the steps you need to follow:

  1. Gather all relevant documentation: Collect all the necessary documents, such as payment summaries, receipts, and bank statements, to accurately report your income and deductions.
  2. Lodge your tax return online: The easiest and fastest way to lodge your tax return is through the ATO’s online services. You can use myTax, a user-friendly online platform that guides you through the process.
  3. Report your income and deductions accurately: Ensure that you report all your income from various sources and claim any eligible deductions. Accuracy is crucial to avoid penalties or incorrect tax assessments.
  4. Pay any outstanding tax liabilities: If you owe any tax, it’s important to pay the amount owed as soon as possible to avoid further interest charges and penalties.
  5. Keep records of your lodgement: After giving your tax return, make sure to keep a copy of the lodgement confirmation for your records.

Common reasons for late tax lodgement

There are several common reasons why individuals may file their tax returns late in Australia. Some of these reasons include:

  1. Lack of organisation: Many people simply procrastinate or do not prioritise their tax obligations, leading to late lodgement.
  2. Complex financial situations: Individuals with complex financial situations, such as multiple income sources or investment properties, may find it challenging to gather all the necessary information and complete their tax returns on time.
  3. Life events: Significant life events, such as illness, family emergencies, or personal crises, can disrupt an individual’s ability to meet the tax deadline.
  4. Lack of knowledge: Some individuals may be unaware of the tax deadlines or the consequences of late lodgement, leading to unintentional non-compliance.

Tips for avoiding late tax lodgement in the future.

To avoid late tax lodgement in the future, consider implementing the following tips:

  1. Stay organised: Keep track of your income and expenses throughout the year to make the tax preparation process more manageable.
  2. Set reminders: Mark the tax deadline on your calendar and set reminders to ensure you don’t forget to lodge your tax return on time.
  3. Seek professional help: Consider engaging a tax agent or accountant to aid you with your tax obligations. They can supply guidance, ensure accuracy, and help you meet the deadlines.
  4. Stay informed: Stay updated with the latest tax regulations and deadlines by regularly checking the ATO’s website or subscribing to their newsletters.
  5. Plan: If you expect any challenges in meeting the tax deadline, such as extended travel or medical procedures, plan and seek an extension if necessary.

Seeking professional help for late tax lodgement

If you find yourself in a situation where you have missed the tax deadline or have outstanding tax returns, seeking professional help can be beneficial. How late can you lodge your tax in Australia? A tax agent or accountant can aid you in navigating the process, at times they can extend the date to the 15th of May the following year, ensuring accuracy in your tax returns, and minimising any potential penalties or interest charges.

Professional tax advisors have extensive knowledge and experience in handling late tax lodgement cases. They can communicate with the ATO on your behalf, negotiate any penalties, and guide you through the necessary steps to rectify your tax affairs. By seeking professional help, you can alleviate the stress and complexity associated with late tax lodgement.

Conclusion: Importance of meeting tax deadlines in Australia

Meeting tax deadlines is of utmost importance in Australia. Not lodging your tax return on time can result in penalties, interest charges, and other consequences. It’s crucial to be aware of the tax deadlines, understand the potential penalties for late lodgement, and take immediate action if you find yourself filing your tax return late. By staying organised, seeking professional help when needed, and staying informed about the latest tax regulations, you can ensure compliance and avoid the stress of late tax lodgement.
