About our fees

Do evenings and Saturdays cost more?

No. Appointments cost the same Monday to Saturday, 9am to 7pm.

When do I pay?

If you are due a refund, our fee can come out of it, so there is nothing to pay upfront. Otherwise you can pay by card or bank transfer once your tax return is ready.
