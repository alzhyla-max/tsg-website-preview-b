Most people think it’s worse than it is

People put off their tax for all sorts of reasons. Life gets busy. A year gets missed, then it feels too late to fix, and one year becomes three. It’s far more common than you’d think, and it isn’t a sign that you’re careless.

The good news: the ATO already has most of your information. Your employers, bank and Centrelink report to them every year. That means we usually need much less from you than you’d expect.

How we catch you up

  1. We check your ATO history. With your permission, we see which years are outstanding, what income was reported and whether there are any debts or letters.
  2. One phone interview covers every year. We go through the gaps together: work expenses, a car, a rental, anything else that matters.
  3. We prepare every tax return, oldest first. You see each year before it is lodged.
  4. We lodge and explain the result. You’ll know exactly where you stand, refunds and any balance, with no guesswork.

Bring whatever you have

You don’t need a box of receipts to get started. Your name, date of birth and address are enough for us to find you in the ATO system. Anything else you have, like old payslips, receipts or a logbook, is a bonus.

One client hadn’t lodged since 2002. We retrieved his records from the ATO archives, rebuilt what was missing and lodged every year. In his case it ended with a refund of around $10,000. Every situation is different, but most people tell us the biggest relief is simply knowing it’s done.

FAQ

Late tax returns: common questions

Can't see yours? Call us. Asking costs nothing.

No silly questions. Really.

How many years back can I lodge?

There's no cut-off for lodging a late tax return. We have caught up clients who had not lodged since 2002. The ATO may only ask for certain years, and we check that with them first so you don't do more than you need to.

I've lost my payment summaries and receipts. Can you still help?

Yes. Employers, banks and Centrelink report most income to the ATO, and we can retrieve those records for you. For deductions, we work with what you have and explain what can still be claimed.

Will I be fined for lodging late?

Not always. The ATO can apply a failure-to-lodge penalty, but where you are owed refunds, or have a good reason, we can ask the ATO to reduce or remove it. We'll tell you where you stand after we check your records.

What if I owe the ATO money?

We'll tell you the amount before anything is lodged, and explain the options. The ATO offers payment plans, and we can help you set one up.

How long does it take?

Most catch-ups are lodged within one to two weeks of your phone interview. It depends on how many years there are and what the ATO already has on file.
